THE OPPORTUNITY GAP

Talent is everywhere. Access isn't yet.

The school didn't create the gap. The community didn't create the gap. Systems created the gap.

Students in our classrooms have the talent and the drive. What they don't always have is access to the careers, resources, and role models they deserve. CAMP AHA shows up at that gap and starts closing it, one classroom, one role model, one student at a time.

Why we show up

Our Mission

To bring career role models into the classrooms of the most underserved public schools, giving students the opportunity to see careers they never knew were possible and the inspiration to pursue them.

Our Vision

A future where every underserved community thrives with a generation of students who have seen what's possible, claimed it as their own, and are shaping positive change in their schools and communities.

HOW IT WORKS

Bridging the gap, every week.

Each school provides a classroom and a champion teacher. The teacher facilitates while role models engage students through guided conversations on the topics that matter.

  1. 1

    The school opens the door

    A partner school gives us a classroom, a weekly time slot, and a champion teacher who knows the students.

  2. 2

    Role models sign up

    Local professionals pick an open session, clear the district background check, and get everything they need to walk in ready.

  3. 3

    They show up and share

    One hour. One career story. Students ask the real questions and see a path they never knew existed.

Every Conversation Starts With a Career

Each role model session centers on one real career. Along the way, these topics come up naturally.

Career Development

Every session centers on one real career. Students hear how a role model got there, what the work looks like day to day, and what it takes to get started.

Characteristics of Leadership

How leadership shows up on the job, at every level, not just at the top.

Financial Literacy

What a career pays, how role models manage their money, and the decisions that build a future.

Health and Wellbeing

How role models take care of themselves so they can show up for their work and the people who count on them.
