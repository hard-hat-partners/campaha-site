One person. One hour.

Engage your team

Partnering with CAMP AHA requires no time commitment from your team. Your investment alone makes the program possible.

That said, anyone on your team is welcome to sign up as a career role model for any session during the year. They show up, share their career story, and leave having made a real impact on a classroom full of students who needed to hear it. Some partners love sending team members in. Others simply invest, and that's more than enough.

Community impact

When your company partners with CAMP AHA, you're putting your name on something that matters. Students know your company showed up for them. Teachers and administrators see it firsthand. Parents hear about it. Your own employees take pride in it too.

That kind of authentic community presence builds trust that no advertising budget can buy.

Heartfelt gratitude

Every partner hears how their support lands. We share real student reflections and moments from the classroom in our newsletter and our Annual Impact Report, so your team sees the careers that opened up because your company showed up.

Community Partners see it firsthand, in the room with the students they stand behind.
