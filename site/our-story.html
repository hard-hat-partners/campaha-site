CAREER ROLE MODELS

Showing up for the next generation.

Career role models show up for students who need to see what's possible. They share their career journeys, offer real guidance, and prove that success is within reach for every kid sitting in that classroom.

  • Seasoned Experience

    They bring real career experience into the classroom, sharing the kind of wisdom that only comes from actually living it.

  • Entrepreneurial Spirit

    Whether they're a few years into a career or a professional who has already made it, their drive is contagious in a room full of students still deciding what's possible.

  • Diverse Skill Sets

    From healthcare to finance, athletics to the arts, our role models represent the full spectrum of what a career can look like.

  • Active Community Engagement

    They show up because they genuinely care about the communities these students come from. Many of them came from those same communities.

  • Leadership and Dedication

    They give their time, share their story, and show up for students who need to hear it most. That is leadership in its purest form.

These role models are not filling a time slot. They are igniting a chain reaction of possibility. Becoming a career role model is a chance to share your story, inspire young minds, and build a future where every student can see what's possible for them.

A VISION FOR GROWTH

Empowering thousands

We envision CAMP AHA bringing career role models into classrooms across underserved communities in Dallas, Houston, and beyond. One school at a time, one career story at a time, we are building a platform that grows without losing what makes it work: simplicity, consistency, and showing up for the students who need it most.

Community Investment Goal

We are seeking community partners and investors to expand our career role model program to more schools, more cities, and more students who deserve to see what's possible for them.

Strategic Growth

We grow deliberately. Each new school belongs to its own ecosystem with a local network of professionals and a community partner. That keeps the program focused, sustainable, and genuinely impactful.

Resource Allocation

As we grow, we add the right people and tools to support each school relationship. Every resource serves one purpose: making sure role models show up and careers become visible where they matter most.

The ripple effect

When a career role model shows up, something shifts in that classroom. A student who never considered medicine, finance, or engineering suddenly sees it as possible. That moment ripples outward into their choices, their confidence, and eventually their community.
