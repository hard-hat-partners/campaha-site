Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

CAMP AHA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that brings career role models into Title I classrooms in Dallas and Houston. We keep this site simple and we respect your privacy. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to share with us: when you email us, when you sign up for a role model session, or when you join our newsletter. That can include your name, email address, phone number, employer, and career field.

No tracking on this site

This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking scripts. Our fonts and images are hosted on our own site, so nothing loads from another company when you visit. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

Services we use

Role model sign ups run through Calendly. Our newsletter runs through MailerLite. Our email runs through Google Workspace. When you use one of these, that company handles your information under its own privacy policy. You leave our site when you click to sign up on Calendly.

How we use your information

We use your information to schedule sessions, send session reminders, share the district volunteer and background check steps, follow up after sessions, and send our newsletter. We never sell, rent, or trade your information.

Students

This site is written for adults: role models, partners, and school leaders. We do not collect information from students through this site. Photos of students appear with permission. To request that a photo be removed, email info@campaha.org and we will take it down.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email. To see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, email info@campaha.org.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@campaha.org.
