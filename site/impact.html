CAREER ROLE MODELS

Going beyond traditional career programs

Across Texas, hundreds of thousands of students attend Title I schools in underserved communities, showing up every day with potential and no roadmap for what their career future could look like. CAMP AHA meets them where they are.

We partner with schools in Dallas ISD and Houston ISD where the need is greatest. These students deserve to see careers that are possible for someone who looks like them and comes from where they come from.

Two cities. One mission.

Did you know, Dallas?
89.7%of Royce West students are economically disadvantaged
85.9%are considered at risk of dropping out

Royce West Leadership Academy sits in 75241 in southern Dallas, one of ten zip codes a 2013 Stand for Children study linked to the city's highest incarceration rates and lowest college readiness. Our students show up every day with real potential. What many have never had is a working professional in the room showing them what's possible. That's where we come in.

Source: Texas Education Agency enrollment data via The Texas Tribune.

Dallas ISD partner: Royce West Leadership Academy
Did you know, Houston?
97.7%of Crispus Attucks students are economically disadvantaged
69%are considered at risk of dropping out

Crispus Attucks STEAM Academy serves the Sunnyside community in south Houston. These students are already putting in the work: the school earned a B from the state and an A for closing achievement gaps. Now imagine what happens when they can see the careers waiting for them.

Source: Texas Education Agency enrollment data via The Texas Tribune.

Investing in our future leaders

CAMP AHA disrupts the cycle by showing up in the classrooms of these very neighborhoods. Every child deserves the chance to see what their future could look like, regardless of their zip code.

Through career role models who share real stories and open real doors, we make careers visible for students who have never had the chance to see them.

POSITIVE GAINS

Quantifying the impact

Every session generates real feedback from role models and school staff. Here is a glimpse of what it shows.

100%

of role models reported a positive experience

A significant number rated it "great." Their enthusiasm shows their commitment to the students and the communities they serve.

Closing the knowledge gap

Surveys show a significant increase in students' self-confidence, leadership skills, and positive outlook on their futures.

Engaged students

Role models consistently see high engagement. Students ask questions, make connections, and discover careers they never considered.

Enhanced personal development

Sessions covering financial literacy, career development, health and wellbeing, and leadership close the gap between where students are and where they could go. Real conversations with real role models make those topics come alive.

The ripple effect of career role models
