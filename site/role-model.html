What role models bring to the classroom

Career Readiness

Every session is a window into a career students have never seen up close. Your story is the curriculum.

Financial Literacy

Real conversations about money, decisions, and building a future emerge naturally from every career journey you share.

Health and Wellbeing

Success starts with taking care of yourself, and role models show what that looks like.

Characteristics of Success

What got you where you are is exactly what these students need to hear. Work ethic, decisions, setbacks, comebacks. All of it.

Who makes a great role model?

  • You have a career story worth sharing

    You don't have to be famous or have all the answers. Show up and be real about where you came from and where you are now.

  • You came from somewhere and made it somewhere

    The most powerful role models are the ones whose story feels familiar to the students in that room.

  • You are on your own path

    Just getting started, a seasoned professional, or an entrepreneur who built your own lane. Every path is valid. Every story matters.

  • You bring a unique perspective

    Every career, background, and life experience adds something different. Your story expands what students believe is possible.

  • You want to make a real impact

    Showing up for one session is a real act of community investment. These students will remember you long after you leave the room.
